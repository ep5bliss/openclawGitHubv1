### Risk Mitigation

**Risk 1: Product doesn't sell**
Mitigation: Launch at $14.99. Even 2–3 sales/week at this price confirms market. Adjust keywords and photos before giving up.

**Risk 2: Tournament delayed/cancelled**
Mitigation: Design product to be tournament-agnostic. "2026 Soccer Season" framing works any time. Remove specific date references from core pages.

**Risk 3: Competition copies the product**
Mitigation: Quality and brand trust are the moat. First-mover with reviews wins. Build email list to create owned audience.

**Risk 4: Etsy algorithm changes**
Mitigation: Build off-Etsy presence (Pinterest, TikTok, email) so you're not dependent on any single platform.
