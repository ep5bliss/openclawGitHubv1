## The product in one line
A premium, illustrated, print-at-home system that teaches KIDS (two age tiers) and PARENTS
how to actually read a Nutrition Facts label and make a decision in under 10 seconds.

## Dual audience — this is THE differentiator
Most products pick one. We ship BOTH, designed to be used together at the same kitchen table:
- **Parent track**: authority, confidence, the "why," how to not raise a food-anxious kid.
- **Kid track**: game-first, story-first, zero lecture. Two tiers — Ages 6–9 and Ages 10–13.

## Non-negotiable quality bar
Benchmark = the top 1% of paid digital products (Big Life Journal, Panda Planner,
Teachers Pay Teachers "Gold" sellers, Lovevery-grade design). If a page would not survive
being printed, laminated, and put on a fridge for a year, it does not ship.

## Hard rules
1. **Factually bulletproof.** Every number traced to FDA/USDA/AAP. No wellness-influencer myths.
2. **No fear, no fat-shaming, no food moralizing.** No "good food / bad food." Teach reading,
   not restriction. This protects kids AND protects the brand from backlash.
3. **No medical advice.** Educational only. Disclaimer required.
4. **Print-first design.** Must look elite in grayscale on a home inkjet AND in full color.
5. **Accessible.** Dyslexia-friendly type option, WCAG AA contrast, plain-language reading levels.
6. Every asset must be reproducible — we ship source files, not just flattened PDFs.
